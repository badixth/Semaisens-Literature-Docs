The platform aggregates imagery from multiple satellite constellations and drone uploads so you always have the best available data for your fields — whether that means free daily Sentinel-2 coverage or sub-meter drone orthomosaics captured during a scouting flight. Each source has different characteristics in terms of resolution, revisit frequency, and cost, and the platform is designed to seamlessly blend them into a unified view of every field you manage.

## Satellite Sources

| Satellite | Provider | Resolution | Revisit Rate | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sentinel-2 | ESA (European Space Agency) | 10 m | ~5 days | Free (included) |
| Landsat 8 / 9 | USGS | 30 m | 16 days | Free (included) |
| Planet PlanetScope | Planet Labs (commercial) | 3 m | Daily | Requires Planet subscription |
| Maxar | Maxar Technologies (commercial) | \< 0.5 m | On demand | Requires Maxar subscription |

## Automatic vs. Manual Imagery

**Automatically ingested (no setup required)**

Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8/9 imagery is ingested automatically for all registered fields at no additional cost. As soon as you create a field boundary, the platform begins pulling available scenes and processing them for vegetation index calculations. Historical imagery going back to Sentinel-2's operational start in 2015 is also available for retrospective analysis.

**Commercial imagery (subscription required)**

Planet and Maxar imagery requires a separate commercial subscription with those providers. Once you have a subscription, connect it to the platform by navigating to **Settings \> Imagery Sources** and entering your API credentials. After connecting, high-resolution imagery will be automatically ingested alongside free sources for all active fields.

<Warning>
  Commercial imagery costs are governed by your agreement with Planet or Maxar and are billed directly by those providers — not through SemaiSens. Connecting a commercial account will cause the platform to make imagery requests on your behalf, which may count against your subscription quota. Review your provider's pricing before enabling automatic high-resolution ingestion.
</Warning>

## Drone Imagery

When satellite resolution is not sufficient for the detail level your scouting requires, you can upload drone-captured orthomosaics directly to the platform. Drone imagery is processed using the same pipeline as satellite imagery — atmospherically corrected where metadata permits — and immediately available as an analysis layer alongside your satellite-derived indices.

**Supported formats and specifications**

| Attribute | Supported Values |
| --- | --- |
| File format | GeoTIFF (`.tif`, `.tiff`) |
| Band configurations | RGB, NDVI single-band, multispectral (5-band) |
| Maximum file size | 2 GB per upload |
| Coordinate system | WGS84 (EPSG:4326) or any EPSG-registered CRS |

**How to upload drone imagery**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field">
    Navigate to the field you want to attach the drone imagery to and open the field detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Imagery tab">
    Select the **Imagery** tab at the top of the field view to see all imagery layers associated with this field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the upload">
    Click **Upload** and select your GeoTIFF file from your local machine. The uploader accepts files up to 2 GB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm geolocation">
    The platform reads the embedded geospatial metadata from the GeoTIFF and displays a preview overlay on the field map. Confirm that the drone imagery aligns correctly with your field boundary, then click **Confirm** to begin processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Processing time depends on file size and band count, typically between 2 and 15 minutes. You will receive an in-app notification and an email when the layer is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Make sure your drone imagery is processed into a georeferenced orthomosaic before uploading. Raw UAV images or non-georeferenced files are not supported. Most drone processing software — including DJI Terra, Pix4D, and Agisoft Metashape — exports analysis-ready GeoTIFFs directly.
</Tip>

## Image Quality & Cloud Cover

Optical satellite imagery is only useful when the sky above your field is clear. The platform automatically filters out scenes with more than **20% cloud cover** over your field boundary, ensuring that cloudy scenes do not contaminate your index time series or trigger false alerts.

If you work in a persistently cloudy region or need to capture data during a cloudy period, you can adjust the cloud cover threshold:

1. Go to **Settings \> Preferences \> Cloud Cover Filter**.
2. Move the slider to your desired maximum cloud cover percentage (0–100%).
3. Save your preferences. The new threshold applies to all future image ingestion for your organization.

<Note>
  Raising the cloud cover threshold allows more images into your time series but increases the risk of cloud-contaminated pixels affecting index values. If you raise the threshold, inspect imagery carefully on the map before including it in prescriptions or reports. Lowering the threshold below 20% may result in longer gaps between available scenes in cloudy climates.
</Note>

## Data Retention

<Note>
  Processed imagery and index layers are retained for **2 years** on Free and Pro plans. **Enterprise** plans include unlimited historical data retention. If you need to preserve data beyond the retention window on a Free or Pro plan, export your imagery and observation time series before the retention period expires.
</Note>
