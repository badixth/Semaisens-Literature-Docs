title: "Cold Snap and Haze Stress in Rubber"

---

Malaysian rubber rarely faces temperature stress but is affected by two atypical events: cool highland nights (Cameron Highlands, some Sarawak upland blocks) and prolonged **transboundary haze** from Sumatra/Kalimantan peat fires (typically Aug to Oct in El Nino years). Haze cuts solar radiation, suppresses photosynthesis, and reduces latex flow across whole regions.

## Cause

- **Cool nights** (\< 18 C sustained) at highland sites (\> 400 m elevation)
- **Transboundary haze** with API/PSI \> 150 for 2\+ weeks
- **Reduced solar radiation** during prolonged overcast periods

## Effect

| Event | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Cold nights | Latex flow reduction (10 to 20%), delayed refoliation |
| Moderate haze (API 100-200) | Solar radiation reduced 10 to 20%, latex flow -5 to -10% |
| Severe haze (API \> 200) | Solar radiation reduced 30 to 50%, latex flow -15 to -30%, delayed wintering recovery |
| Prolonged haze (\> 3 weeks) | Canopy thinning, elevated CLF risk (weakened trees) |

Field signs:

- Latex flow drop across the whole estate (not localized)
- Grey ash deposit on canopy after major haze episodes
- Delayed refoliation in the following season
- Reduced DRC (dry rubber content)

## Satellite signals

| Index | What to watch | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI | Slight dip during haze | Small, region-wide |
| PAR (photosynthetically active radiation) | Direct drop | Best indicator; use ERA5 or MODIS |
| Aerosol optical depth (AOD) | Direct haze measure | Confirms cause |
| LST | Suppressed daytime temperature | Confirms overcast |

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the cause">
    Check API/PSI readings and satellite AOD. Haze is region-wide, not block-specific, so if only one block is affected, look elsewhere (drought, disease).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reduce tapping intensity">
    During prolonged haze, switch from d/2 to d/3 to conserve tree reserves and prevent TPD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Postpone stimulation">
    Do not apply ethephon during haze; trees cannot recover the stored photosynthates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor for CLF post-haze">
    Weakened trees are more susceptible to [Corynespora leaf fall](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/corynespora) in the refoliation window following haze.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haze | API \> 150 for 7\+ days | Medium |
| Severe haze | API \> 200 for 7\+ days | High |
| Solar radiation | PAR \< 70% of baseline for 14\+ days | Medium |
| Cold night (highland only) | \< 18 C for 5\+ consecutive nights | Low |
