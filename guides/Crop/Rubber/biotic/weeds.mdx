Weeds compete with rubber for water, nutrients, and light, most critically on **immature blocks (year 0 to 4)** before canopy closure. After canopy closure (year 5 to 6), most weeds die back naturally under the shade. Cover crops (Mucuna, Pueraria, Calopogonium) are the first line of defense and reduce weed pressure by 70 to 90% on new plantings.

## Main problem weeds

| Weed | Type | Where | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _Imperata cylindrica_ (lalang) | Grass, perennial rhizomatous | All environments | Aggressive, fire risk, hard to eradicate |
| _Mikania micrantha_ (mile-a-minute) | Vine, climbing | All environments | Smothers young trees, chokes canopy |
| _Asystasia gangetica_ (chinese violet) | Broadleaf, ground-covering | All environments | Ground cover dominance, moderate competition |
| _Chromolaena odorata_ (Siam weed) | Broadleaf, shrub | All environments | Tall shrub, competes for light |
| _Melastoma malabathricum_ (senduduk) | Shrub | Acid, wet soils | Persistent on marginal blocks |
| Sedges (_Cyperus_ spp.) | Sedge | Wet blocks | Moisture indicator, moderate competition |

## Effect

| Stand age | Weed impact |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 1 year | Severe. Weeds smother saplings, cause up to 50% establishment loss if unmanaged |
| 1 to 3 years | Moderate. Competition slows girth increment by 20 to 40% |
| 3 to 5 years | Low. Canopy closing, weeds shaded out except in gaps |
| 5\+ years | Minimal in closed stands; localized problem in gaps and on paths |

## Cover-crop-first approach

Malaysian rubber best practice: plant a **legume cover crop mixture** at planting.

- **Mucuna bracteata** (dominant modern choice, fast growth, shade-tolerant)
- **Pueraria phaseoloides** (traditional, still widely used)
- **Calopogonium mucunoides**, **Centrosema pubescens** (companion species)

Well-established cover crops:

- Suppress 70 to 90% of weeds through competition and shade
- Fix 100 to 200 kg N/ha/year
- Reduce erosion on sloping blocks
- Reduce weed-control cost by ~60% in years 1 to 3

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Establish cover crop at planting">
    Sow the legume mixture at planting or immediately after clearing. Broadcast at 10 to 15 kg/ha total seed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Circle weeding of tree bases">
    Keep a 1 m radius clean circle around each tree in years 1 to 3. Manual weeding or targeted glyphosate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interrow spot treatment">
    Spot-spray glyphosate on lalang and Mikania patches. Do not blanket-spray or you kill the cover crop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Special measures for Imperata (lalang)">
    Lalang requires repeated treatment. Options: repeated glyphosate applications, plastic mulching, deep discing before replanting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mikania control">
    Cut Mikania away from the sapling manually every 6 to 8 weeks in years 1 to 3. Systemic herbicide (glyphosate wick-wipe) on established patches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discontinue routine weeding after canopy closure">
    Once canopy closes (year 5 to 6), only spot-treat gaps and path edges. Whole-block spraying is unnecessary and expensive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immature-block NDVI | \< age-band baseline sustained 30 days on year 0-3 block | Medium |
| Scout report | Lalang or Mikania coverage \> 20% on any immature block | Medium |
| Cover crop failure | Legume ground cover \< 50% at 6 months post-planting | High |
