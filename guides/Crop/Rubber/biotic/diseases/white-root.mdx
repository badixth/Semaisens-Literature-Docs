**White root disease** (_Rigidoporus microporus_, syn. _Rigidoporus lignosus_) is the number-one killer of Malaysian rubber. It spreads by root-to-root contact and by contact with infected stumps, forming expanding circular foci that can wipe out entire blocks if not stopped early. It is the top priority for the [Field Walk Protocol](/guides/Crop/Rubber/field-walk-protocol) root-collar inspection every quarter.

## Cause

- **Old rubber stumps** left in the ground at replanting (primary reservoir)
- **Adjacent forest or old rubber** at block boundaries
- **Root contact** with an infected neighbor
- **Waterlogging** weakens tree defenses and accelerates infection
- **Wound sites** on roots from ploughing or machinery

## Effect

| Stage | Above-ground symptom | Below-ground symptom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | No visible above-ground symptom | White rhizomorph fans on root surface |
| Mid | Yellowing canopy on one side of the tree | Rhizomorphs spread up the root collar, root rot begins |
| Late | Wilting, defoliation, latex flow drop | Root system rotten, tree death within 6 to 12 months |
| Terminal | Tree dead, fruiting bodies (orange bracket fungi) at collar base | Complete root destruction, adjacent trees already infected |

The disease spreads at 1 to 3 m per year from tree to tree. A focus that starts with one tree grows to 5 to 10 dead trees within 2 years if not stopped.

## Satellite signals

| Index | What to watch | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI high-res | Localized declining cluster, expanding month-to-month | Direct observation |
| Anomaly view | Same-block year-over-year, expanding low-NDVI patch | Direct observation |

Cross-check any expanding cluster against the [rubber ecosystems](/guides/Crop/Rubber/rubber-ecosystems) baseline to distinguish white root from wintering.

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm at the collar">
    Scrape topsoil at the collar of the suspect tree. White fan-shaped rhizomorphs on the root surface confirm _Rigidoporus microporus_. If unsure, contact the Malaysian Rubber Board for confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cordon isolation trench">
    Dig an isolation trench (60 cm deep, 30 cm wide) around the focus, at least 2 m outside the last infected tree. This severs root contact and stops horizontal spread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect every neighbor within 5 m">
    Scrape collar of every tree within 5 m in all directions from the infected focus. Root grafts spread the pathogen invisibly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fell, uproot, and burn infected trees">
    Fell all confirmed and probable trees. **Uproot the stumps** (do not leave them in the ground; they are the primary reservoir). Burn all root material on site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply fungicide to remaining neighbors">
    Drench root collar of neighbors within 5 m with tridemorph (Calixin) or triadimefon (Bayleton) as per MRB recommendation. Repeat every 6 months for 2 years.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sanitize tools">
    Wash all tools with fungicide or bleach between blocks. Never move root material or stump wood off-site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Re-inspect the treated zone every 3 months for 2 years. Expanding-focus surveillance is the only way to catch missed cases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expanding cluster (satellite) | Localized NDVI decline expanding \> 1 tree per month | High |
| Scout confirmation | White rhizomorphs recorded on any tree | Critical |
| Boundary sweep | Automatic quarterly reminder for old-stump lines and forest edges | Automatic |
| Follow-up inspection | 3-month cadence for 2 years after any confirmed case | Automatic |
