**Pestalotiopsis / Neopestalotiopsis leaf fall** has emerged as a major threat to Malaysian, Indonesian, and Thai rubber since around 2017. In Indonesia, it has caused catastrophic latex losses (estimates of 20 to 45% yield reduction in outbreak areas). Symptoms overlap heavily with [Corynespora leaf fall](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/corynespora), so ground confirmation is essential for species-level diagnosis.

## Cause

- _Neopestalotiopsis cubana_ and related _Pestalotiopsis_ species
- **High humidity** and warm temperatures
- **Continuous rain** during refoliation
- **Clonal susceptibility** (still being characterized; many clones affected)
- **Cross-border spread** from Indonesia and Thailand into Malaysia

## Effect

| Stage | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| Early | Small tan-brown spots (1 to 3 mm) with grey-white centers |
| Mid | Spots may develop "shot-hole" or needle-hole appearance |
| Late | Whole-leaf blight, premature leaf fall out of wintering window |
| Terminal | Repeated defoliation, severe canopy loss, latex flow crash |

How to distinguish from CLF:

- Pestalotiopsis lesions are typically **smaller** with **grey-white centers**
- CLF has larger lesions with **fishbone/railway veinal patterns**
- Both cause out-of-season leaf fall; laboratory confirmation is the only certain method

## Satellite signals

Same pattern as CLF: out-of-season NDVI drop and delayed refoliation. Satellite cannot distinguish the two; ground diagnosis required.

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send samples for identification">
    Contact the Malaysian Rubber Board (MRB) for confirmation. Symptoms mimic CLF; laboratory identification is required. Report to MRB regardless (regulatory tracking).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fungicide options (research ongoing)">
    Current best options include **hexaconazole**, **propiconazole**, and **mancozeb** on immature trees. Effectiveness is variable; follow current MRB advisories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reduce tapping intensity">
    Same principle as CLF: allow refoliation, do not compound stress with over-tapping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor spread">
    Track new outbreaks; report to MRB to support regional surveillance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

Same as CLF (same satellite signature). Distinguish species by ground diagnosis.

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Out-of-season NDVI drop | -0.10 sustained 14 days outside Feb-Apr window | High |
| Regional advisory | MRB or IRRDB outbreak advisory triggers heightened scouting | High |
