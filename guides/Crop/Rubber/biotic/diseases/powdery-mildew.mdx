**Powdery mildew** (_Oidium heveae_) attacks rubber during the refoliation window immediately after wintering. It kills young leaves as they emerge, prolonging the annual NDVI dip by 2 to 4 weeks and causing repeated flush loss. Most damaging in northern Peninsular states (Kedah, Perlis, Perak) where wintering coincides with cool, misty mornings.

## Cause

- **Cool nights (\< 22 C) with misty mornings** during refoliation (Feb-Apr)
- **Susceptible clones**: RRIM 600, some PB clones
- **Northern states** with more pronounced dry-cool wintering
- **Highland blocks** (\> 300 m elevation)

## Effect

| Stage | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| Early | White powdery growth on the underside of young leaves |
| Mid | Leaves distorted, curled, blackened at margins |
| Late | Whole flush dies back, tree must produce a second flush |
| Terminal | Repeated flush loss, delayed canopy recovery, delayed tapping resumption |

## Satellite signals

| Index | What to watch | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI | Prolonged wintering trough | Recovery \> 10 weeks (vs normal 6 to 8) |
| Anomaly view | Delayed refoliation vs prior year on same block | Direct comparison |

A slow post-wintering recovery in northern states is the diagnostic satellite signal for powdery mildew.

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm on new flush">
    White powder on the underside of expanding leaves confirms _Oidium heveae_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fungicide sprays (timing-critical)">
    Begin sprays **at start of refoliation** (first sign of new flush). Late sprays are ineffective.

    - **Sulphur dust** (30 kg/ha via mist blower), 3 to 5 rounds at 7 to 10 day intervals
    - Alternative: **hexaconazole** or **triadimefon**

    Northern states typically require the full 5-round program in outbreak years.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust tapping">
    Do not resume tapping until the second flush is fully expanded. Premature tapping on partially-refoliated trees causes long-term yield loss.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Long-term: clone selection">
    On chronic northern blocks, favor mildew-tolerant clones at replanting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delayed refoliation | NDVI recovery \> 10 weeks after leaf fall onset | High |
| Weather trigger | Nighttime T \< 22 C \+ RH \> 90% during Feb-Apr on northern blocks | Medium |
| Northern-block reminder | Automatic Feb 1 spray-program reminder for northern estates | Automatic |
