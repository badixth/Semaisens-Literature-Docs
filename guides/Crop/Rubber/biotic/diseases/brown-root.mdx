**Brown root disease** (_Phellinus noxius_) is the third of Malaysia's three major rubber root diseases. It is less common than white or red root but persists in old estate blocks and re-emerges at replanting. Slower spread but similar cordon-and-destroy management.

## Cause

- **Old stumps** in replanting blocks
- **Long-cycle estates** with multiple rubber rotations
- **Adjacent forest edges**

## Effect

| Stage | Above-ground symptom | Below-ground symptom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | None visible | Dark brown crust with soil particles firmly encrusted on the root surface |
| Mid | Yellowing, thinning canopy | Crust thickens, root rot |
| Late | Wilting, latex drop, tree death | Roots destroyed |

The key diagnostic is the **soil-encrusted crust** on the roots that resists brushing off.

## Satellite signals

Same expanding-cluster pattern as white and red root, but slower (0.3 to 0.5 m/year).

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm at the collar">
    Dark brown crust with adhering soil that cannot be brushed off confirms _Phellinus noxius_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cordon and destroy">
    Same protocol as [white root](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/white-root).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    3-month cadence for 2 years.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

Same as [white root](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/white-root).
