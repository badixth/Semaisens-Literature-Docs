**Termites** (mainly _Coptotermes curvignathus_) are a serious pest of Malaysian rubber, especially on immature blocks (year 1 to 5) and on blocks near forest edges or old stumps. They tunnel into the root collar and trunk from the inside, often killing young trees before external symptoms appear.

## Cause

- **Old rubber stumps** or dead wood in the block (primary nesting sites)
- **Adjacent forest** or reserves
- **Newly-planted blocks** with tender bark
- **Dry season** stress that weakens tree defenses
- **Poor land preparation** (stumps left in the ground)

## Effect

| Stand age | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 years | Sapling collar hollowed, tree snaps at ground level, high mortality |
| 3 to 7 years | Trunk galleries, weakened structure, wind-throw risk |
| Mature | Rarely fatal but reduces girth and yield |

Field signs:

- **Mud tubes** on the trunk running up from the ground (diagnostic)
- **Hollow sound** when tapping the trunk with a knife handle
- **Hollowed collar** revealed by scraping
- Sudden wilt of a young tree with no root disease sign
- Sawdust-like frass at the base

## Satellite signals

On immature blocks, cluster mortality shows as bare patches. Not detectable at Sentinel scale on mature stands.

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm on the trunk">
    Mud tubes on the trunk are diagnostic. Scrape the collar of any wilted young tree to confirm hollowing and termite presence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Baiting stations (preferred)">
    Install termite bait stations (fipronil or chlorfluazuron-based commercial products) at the base of affected trees and around the perimeter. Bait is the modern standard because it eliminates the colony, not just individual foragers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soil drench (severe outbreaks)">
    For severe outbreaks, drench soil around the collar with **fipronil** or **imidacloprid** at label rates. Follow current MRB advisories and PPE requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove nesting reservoirs">
    Uproot and burn old stumps, dead wood, and cut logs. This is the single most important preventive measure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boundary buffer">
    On blocks adjacent to forest, maintain a bait-station line along the boundary and inspect quarterly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout report | Mud tubes on any tree | High |
| Immature cluster | \> 2 wilted year 0-3 trees in a 10-tree sample | High |
| Boundary inspection | Automatic quarterly reminder for forest-edge blocks | Automatic |
