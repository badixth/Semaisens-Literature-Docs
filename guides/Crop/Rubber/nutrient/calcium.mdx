title: "Calcium Deficiency in Rubber"

---

Calcium deficiency in rubber is rare as a primary nutrient issue but common as a symptom of soil acidity. Most Malaysian rubber soils are acid (pH 4 to 5), and Ca correction usually happens as a byproduct of liming for pH and Mg management. Ca is essential for cell wall formation and healthy bark, so deficiency affects tapping-panel quality and bark renewal.

## Cause

- **Acid soils** (pH \< 5) with low base saturation
- **Sandy and peat soils** with low Ca reserves
- **Heavy K and Mg fertilization** displacing Ca on the soil exchange
- **No liming** for many years

## Effect

Field signs:

- Young leaves in the top whorl show interveinal chlorosis, green veins on yellow leaf (Ca is immobile, deficiency shows on new growth)
- Distorted new flush, marginal leaf necrosis
- Poor bark renewal on the tapping panel (thin, cracked, slow-regenerating bark)
- Weak root system, elevated root-disease risk

## Leaf-tissue thresholds

Sample **Whorl 2** leaves.

| Ca level (% dry weight) | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| \< 0.5 | Deficient |
| 0.5 to 0.7 | Marginal |
| 0.7 to 1.0 | Optimal |
| \> 1.0 | High |

## Satellite signals

No direct satellite signal for Ca. Manage via routine soil pH and Whorl 2 tissue tests.

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test soil pH and base saturation">
    Confirm with soil analysis (target pH 5.0 to 5.5, base saturation \> 30%).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply ground magnesium limestone (dolomite)">
    Dolomite (~30% CaO, ~20% MgO) supplies both Ca and Mg and raises pH:

    - Every 3 to 5 years: 500 kg to 1 t/ha broadcast across the block
    - New planting: 250 to 500 g dolomite per planting hole
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alternative: ground limestone (calcite)">
    If Mg is already adequate, use pure ground limestone (~40% CaO). Common on peat blocks where Mg is separately corrected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch bark renewal on tapped panels">
    Ca-deficient trees have poor bark renewal; the new bark is thin and prone to cracking. This opens the tree to [black stripe](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/black-stripe) and [pink disease](/guides/Crop/Rubber/biotic/diseases/pink-disease).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leaf tissue Ca | \< 0.5% on scheduled sample | Medium |
| Soil pH | \< 4.5 | Medium |
| Bark renewal | Scout reports thin/cracked new bark on tapped panel | Medium |
| Liming reminder | 3-year cadence per block | Automatic |
