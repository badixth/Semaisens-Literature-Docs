---
title: "Nitrogen Deficiency in Rubber"
sidebarTitle: "Nitrogen (N)"
description: "How nitrogen deficiency presents in Hevea rubber, the Whorl 2 leaf-tissue thresholds, and the corrective fertilization schedule for immature and mature stands."
---

Nitrogen drives canopy build in immature rubber and sustained latex synthesis in mature stands. Deficiency shows first as uniform pale-green canopy across the whole tree, and satellite signals appear before ground symptoms are obvious.

## Cause

- **Immature blocks** without cover crop or fertilizer program
- **Leaching** on sandy soils after heavy rain
- **Peat blocks** with N locked in organic matter
- **Poor cover crop establishment** (Mucuna, Pueraria, Calopogonium)
- **Over-tapping** depleting stored N

## Effect

| Stand age | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 years | Slow growth, uniform pale-green canopy, delayed canopy closure |
| 3 to 5 years | Delayed opening (year 6-7 instead of year 5) |
| 5\+ years (tapped) | Reduced latex flow, lower DRC, thin canopy |

Field signs:

- Whole canopy uniformly pale green to yellow-green
- Older leaves yellowing first, then progressing to whole canopy
- Reduced leaf size on new flush
- Slower girth increment (immature) or reduced tapping yield (mature)

## Leaf-tissue thresholds

Sample **Whorl 2** leaves (second whorl from top, fully-expanded, 3 to 4 months old), 20 trees per block.

| N level (% dry weight) | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| \< 3.0 | Deficient |
| 3.0 to 3.3 | Marginal |
| 3.3 to 3.7 | Optimal |
| \> 3.7 | High |

## Satellite signals

| Index | What to watch | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI | Whole-block low reading | -0.05 vs baseline for age band |
| NDRE | Sensitive to chlorophyll drop | -0.03 sustained |
| Chlorophyll index (CIred-edge) | Direct chlorophyll estimate | Below baseline |

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm with leaf tissue">
    Sample Whorl 2 leaves before fertilizing. Blanket application without confirmation wastes money on well-supplied blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply corrective fertilizer">
    Use urea (46% N) or ammonium sulphate (21% N) split into 2 or 3 applications per year.

    - Immature (year 1-5): 100 to 200 g urea/tree/year, split
    - Mature (year 6\+): 200 to 400 g urea/tree/year, split

    Adjust for MRB clonal recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain cover crop on immature blocks">
    Mucuna and Pueraria fix 100 to 200 kg N/ha/year and reduce fertilizer need in years 1 to 3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Re-sample Whorl 2 leaves 3 months after fertilizer application. Expect NDRE recovery within 6 to 8 weeks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI whole-block low | -0.05 vs age-band baseline sustained 21 days | Medium |
| NDRE low | -0.03 sustained 21 days | Medium |
| Leaf tissue N | \< 3.0% on scheduled sample | High |
| Annual reminder | Whorl 2 sampling reminder every 6 months | Automatic |
