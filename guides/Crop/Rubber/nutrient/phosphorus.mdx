title: "Phosphorus Deficiency in Rubber"
sidebarTitle: "Phosphorus (P)"

---

Phosphorus is the key nutrient for root development and girth increment in immature rubber. Most Malaysian tropical soils are P-fixing (acid, high in Fe/Al oxides), so P is often the limiting factor on new plantings, even where total soil P is adequate.

## Cause

- **Acid soils** (pH \< 5) with high P fixation by Fe and Al oxides
- **Peat soils** with P bound in organic form
- **No P fertilizer** at planting (rock phosphate application missed)
- **Erosion** on hilly blocks removing topsoil

## Effect

| Stand age | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 years | Slow girth increment, poor root development, delayed canopy build |
| 3 to 5 years | Delayed opening for tapping (year 7-8 instead of year 5-6) |
| 5\+ years | Chronically low yield, thin canopy |

Field signs:

- Small dark-green leaves (as opposed to N deficiency which is pale green)
- Reduced leaf size on new flush
- Purple-red tint on older leaves in severe cases
- Poor root development visible at collar scraping
- Slow girth increment (measure at 150 cm; \< 5 cm/year on immature blocks flags P deficiency)

## Leaf-tissue thresholds

Sample **Whorl 2** leaves.

| P level (% dry weight) | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| \< 0.20 | Deficient |
| 0.20 to 0.23 | Marginal |
| 0.23 to 0.28 | Optimal |
| \> 0.28 | High |

## Satellite signals

P deficiency has no strong satellite signal. Diagnosis is by tissue analysis and girth measurement. Slow canopy build on immature blocks is the main indirect signal.

| Index | What to watch | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDVI (immature) | Slow rise vs age-band baseline | -0.05 for age |
| Canopy closure age | \> 6 years vs expected 5 | Direct measure |

## Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm with tissue and soil test">
    P deficiency is confirmed by Whorl 2 tissue \< 0.20% combined with soil P (Bray-1) \< 15 ppm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply rock phosphate at planting">
    Best practice: 250 to 500 g rock phosphate per planting hole, mixed with topsoil.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Corrective application on established blocks">
    Triple super phosphate (TSP) or Christmas Island rock phosphate:

    - Immature: 100 to 200 g TSP/tree/year
    - Mature: 100 to 150 g TSP/tree/year (P demand is low once canopy is established)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address soil acidity">
    Lime application (500 kg to 1 t/ha ground magnesium limestone) raises pH and reduces P fixation. Especially important on peat and very acid mineral soils.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended alert settings

| Trigger | Condition | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leaf tissue P | \< 0.20% on scheduled sample | Medium |
| Girth increment | \< 5 cm/year on year 2-4 block | Medium |
| Soil pH | \< 4.5 on scheduled soil test | Medium |
| Canopy closure delayed | Year 6\+ without closure on mineral upland block | Medium |
