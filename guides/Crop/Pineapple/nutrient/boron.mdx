Boron is required for cell division and pollen viability. On pineapple, deficiency shows as **cracked and corky young leaves** and **cracked, misshapen fruit** with internal cavities. B is easily leached from sandy soils and locked up at high pH.

**Regional priority:** Moderate on sandy, BRIS, and calcareous soils. Under-diagnosed in many operations because symptoms overlap with Ca and fruit borer damage.

## When pineapple is most vulnerable

At **forcing (month 12 to 14)** and again at **fruit set (month 14 to 16)** - the two windows where cell division is most active in the developing inflorescence and fruit.

## How boron deficiency shows in satellite data

- Not detectable at Sentinel scale.
- Confirm by D-leaf analysis and fruit inspection at harvest.

## Field symptoms

- **Young leaves cracked, corky, or brittle** - snap when bent
- **Fruit with corky patches and skin cracks**
- **Internal cavities** and misshapen "knobby" fruit
- **Poor sucker and slip production** in chronic deficiency
- **Overlap with Ca deficiency** on fruit - both cause cracking. B affects both young leaves and fruit; Ca mainly affects fruit and older-leaf edges.

## Reduce the damage by

- **Foliar borax or Solubor (0.2%)** applied to the leaf axil at forcing and again at fruit set.
- **Broadcast borax at planting** on known deficient blocks - 5 to 15 kg/ha, incorporated. Do not exceed 20 kg/ha; B toxicity is a real risk.
- **Do not tank-mix B with foliar Ca** at high concentrations - they can precipitate. Apply separately.
- **Keep soil pH below 6.5** to keep B available.
- **D-leaf analysis** - target 20 to 40 ppm B in the D-leaf. This is a generic target with no site, soil, cultivar or season qualifier, so it supports _inside_ or _outside_ and no finer grade — see [Reading a tissue analysis](/snippets/tissue-diagnosis).

<Warning>
  Boron has a narrow window between deficiency and toxicity. Follow label rates strictly. B toxicity shows as leaf tip and margin burn similar to salinity.
</Warning>

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. B deficiency shows at forcing and fruit set, so build the alert around scheduled foliar reminders.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for boron on pineapple:

- **Foliar B reminder** - schedule sprays at forcing (month 12 to 13) and fruit set (month 14 to 15) on all blocks; skip only where D-leaf B is above 30 ppm.
- **D-leaf sampling reminder** - month 10, before forcing.
- **Fruit-quality tracking** - flag blocks with cull rate above 8% for cracked or knobby fruit.
