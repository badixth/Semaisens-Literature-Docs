Thrips (mainly **Frankliniella and Thrips species**) rasp leaf and fruit surface tissue and feed on the released cell contents. On pineapple the main damage is **silver flecking on leaves and fruit**, plus **fruit scarring** and **transmission of Pineapple Yellow Spot Virus (PYSV)** in some regions. Thrips populations spike in hot, dry weather.

**Regional priority:** High in Hawaii, Philippines, Taiwan, and Australia (yellow spot virus vector). Moderate elsewhere as a direct feeding pest.

## When pineapple is most vulnerable

At **forcing to fruit set (months 12 to 15)** - thrips feeding at floral induction causes fruit scars and knobs that persist to harvest. Also at **early vegetative** where PYSV is a concern.

## How thrips show in satellite data

- Not detectable at Sentinel scale.
- Population dynamics track dry-hot weather; use weather forecast as the leading indicator.

## Field symptoms

- **Silver flecking** on leaf surfaces and fruit shells
- **Small, black frass dots** on the flecked surfaces
- **Fruit scarring and knobbing** if feeding was heavy at fruit set
- **Distorted young leaves** in advanced infestation
- **Yellow spot symptoms** where PYSV is present - yellow leaf spots that expand to necrotic patches, plant collapse
- **Best confirmed** by tapping a leaf whorl over a white tray and counting the small yellow-brown insects

## Reduce the damage by

- **Pre-forcing spray** - a single spinetoram or abamectin application before floral induction protects the developing fruit.
- **Scout with white tray beat sampling** every 2 weeks during dry weather.
- **Threshold** - action at more than 5 thrips per whorl during forcing to fruit-set window.
- **Silver mulch** deters thrips landing compared with black mulch.
- **Rogue PYSV-symptomatic plants** promptly in yellow-spot regions to reduce virus source.
- **Rotate modes of action** (spinetoram, abamectin, methomyl) to slow resistance; thrips resistance develops fast.
- **Avoid broad-spectrum sprays** that kill thrips predators (Orius, predatory mites).

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. Thrips peak in hot-dry weather, so combine a weather alert with a scheduled pre-forcing spray reminder.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for thrips on pineapple:

- **Weather alert** - 10 or more consecutive days above 30 C with rainfall below 20 mm triggers a thrips scout.
- **Pre-forcing reminder** - schedule a thrips spray 1 week before every forcing application.
- **PYSV follow-up alert** - trigger a rogue-and-record walk if yellow-spot symptoms are found on any plant.
