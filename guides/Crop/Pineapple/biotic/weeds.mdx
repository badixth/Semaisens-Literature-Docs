Weeds cost pineapple yield through direct competition (water, nutrients, light) and as reservoirs for **mealybugs**, **thrips**, and **nematodes**. Pineapple's slow canopy development (10 to 12 months to close) makes weed control critical for the entire first year of the cycle. Plastic mulch handles most of the in-row problem; the between-row and headland problem is where the yield loss and vector risk sit.

**Regional priority:** Universal. Highest on newly-cleared land, ratoon blocks, and any block with damaged mulch.

## When pineapple is most vulnerable

From **establishment (0 to 3 months)** through **canopy closure (month 10 to 12)**. Weed damage after canopy closure is minor because the pineapple canopy shades most weeds.

## How weed pressure shows in satellite data

- **NDVI above phase baseline** in early months (weeds add greenness) then a drop when weeds are killed or the pineapple falls behind.
- **Patchy NDVI** where mulch has failed and weeds are dense.
- Distinguishable from healthy pineapple by texture on high-resolution imagery.
- Difficult to separate from establishment failure without ground truth.

## Field symptoms

- **Weeds visible between rows and in the furrow**
- **Weeds emerging through torn plastic mulch**
- **Poor pineapple growth in weedy zones**
- **Mealybug and ant activity** in weedy patches
- **Grass and broadleaf weeds** on headlands provide thrips reservoirs
- **Volunteer pineapple** from previous cycle

## Reduce the damage by

- **Plastic mulch as the primary in-row control** - install carefully and repair tears immediately.
- **Pre-emergence herbicides** on furrows and between rows - diuron, bromacil, or ametryn (subject to registration).
- **Post-emergence herbicides** - glyphosate (directed away from pineapple) or fluazifop for grass. **Paraquat, the traditional shielded-spray option, has been banned in Malaysia since 2020** - see [Pesticide registration](/snippets/pesticide-registration).
- **Hand-weeding** in the leaf axil and around the plant base where herbicide cannot reach.
- **Cover crops on headlands** - low-growing species that outcompete weeds without harboring pests.
- **Manage headlands** monthly - mow or spray to prevent seed set.
- **Bare-ground barrier** between old and new blocks reduces mealybug and ant migration.
- **Do not compost weeds** with mealybugs or thrips into the block; burn or remove.

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Weed monitoring is best done with scheduled scouting and mulch inspection. Satellite alerts help detect early canopy failure that may be weed competition.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for weeds on pineapple:

- **Mulch inspection reminder** - monthly for the first 6 months, then quarterly.
- **Herbicide-application reminder** - schedule pre-emergence at planting and follow-up post-emergence at 3 and 6 months.
- **NDVI anomaly alert** - trigger on patchy low NDVI in the first 3 months (establishment failure or weed pressure).
- **Headland-management reminder** - monthly mowing or spraying reminder for the entire cycle.
