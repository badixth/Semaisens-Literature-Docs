Pineapple nematodes are a slow, quiet killer of yield. **Root-knot (Meloidogyne)**, **reniform (Rotylenchulus reniformis)**, and **lesion (Pratylenchus)** nematodes damage the root system, cause a slow diffuse block-wide decline, and are the main reason ratoon and replant blocks yield less than first-cycle blocks.

**Regional priority:** Very high on replant and ratoon blocks worldwide. Universal on sandy and BRIS soils.

## When pineapple is most vulnerable

From **establishment (month 0 to 3)** onward, worsening with each cycle. Damage is chronic and multiplicative if blocks are replanted without a fallow break.

## How nematodes show in satellite data

- **Slow, diffuse NDVI shortfall** over months - no clear pattern.
- **Block-wide** rather than patchy.
- **Same block flags cycle after cycle** if replanted without fallow.
- Very hard to distinguish from chronic nutrient deficiency without soil and root sampling.

## Field symptoms

- **Stunted plants** with short D-leaves
- **Slow, diffuse block-wide decline** over months
- **Roots stunted, thick, and twisted** with knots (root-knot) or short and dark (lesion, reniform)
- **Few feeder roots** for the plant's age
- **Reduced fruit size** and yield
- **Poor response to fertilizer** despite adequate soil test values
- **Overlap with waterlogging** - separate by drainage pattern; nematodes are block-wide, waterlogging is zone-specific

## Reduce the damage by

- **Soil and root sampling before replanting** - nematode counts guide the fallow decision.
- **Fallow with grass** (Brachiaria, Panicum) or legume for 6 to 12 months between cycles.
- **Cover crops** with nematicidal roots (marigold, mucuna, sunn hemp) plow-under.
- **Hot water treatment of suckers** (50 C, 30 min) kills nematodes in planting material.
- **Nematicide at planting** on chronic blocks - fenamiphos, oxamyl, or fluopyram where approved.
- **Do not replant immediately** after a heavily infected block - the yield loss will compound.
- **Rotate to non-host crops** where possible (grass, cereals) rather than back-to-back pineapple.

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. Nematode damage is chronic and cycle-scale, so use anomaly alerts and scheduled soil sampling.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for nematodes on pineapple:

- **Anomaly alert** - trigger on persistent (\> 90 days) block-wide NDVI shortfall of 5% or more below the phase baseline.
- **Pre-replant soil sampling reminder** - schedule nematode counts 3 months before any planned replant.
- **Fallow-tracking flag** - mark blocks that were replanted with less than 6 months fallow for close monitoring.
