Root rot, caused mainly by **Phytophthora cinnamomi**, is the slower, chronic sibling of heart rot. Instead of collapsing the growing point after a wet event, it rots the root system and lower stem over weeks, causing plants to tip over. It is a long-term drainage problem, not a single-event problem.

**Regional priority:** High on flat, poorly drained blocks; peat blocks with poor canal maintenance; and any block with a history of heart rot.

## When pineapple is most vulnerable

All phases, but visible losses peak at **late vegetative and fruit development** when the fruit weight tips over the compromised root plate.

## How root rot shows in satellite data

- **Chronic NDVI shortfall** in low-lying zones - the same zones flag cycle after cycle.
- **Anomaly view** shows a persistent lower-than-block-average zone.
- Less sudden than heart rot; slower onset, longer decline.
- Confirm on the ground with heart pull (firm) plus root inspection (black, mushy).

## Field symptoms

- **Tipped-over fruiting plants** with root plate lifted
- **Black, mushy, few feeder roots** when the plant is uprooted
- **Basal stem soft and dark** but heart may still be intact (distinguishes from heart rot)
- **Chronic gap zones** in the same low-lying areas across cycles
- **Poor sucker production** on affected plants
- **Overlap with fusariosis** - separate by pattern (root rot follows drainage; fusariosis follows wounds and headlands)

## Reduce the damage by

- **Fix drainage permanently** - this is a chronic drainage problem, not a spray problem. Lateral drains, raised beds, cleared canals.
- **Replant chronic zones on raised profile beds** at least 30 cm above the furrow.
- **Foliar phosphonate** every 2 to 3 months on high-risk blocks - systemic, protects roots.
- **Metalaxyl or fosetyl-Al soil drench** at planting on known infected blocks.
- **Use disease-free planting material** - dip suckers before planting.
- **Break the cycle** with a grass or legume rotation on chronic blocks before replanting.
- **Avoid compaction** - compacted layers hold water at the root zone.

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. Root rot is chronic and zone-specific, so use the anomaly view rather than event alerts.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for root rot on pineapple:

- **Anomaly alert** - trigger on persistent (\> 60 days) NDVI anomaly in a specific zone across cycles.
- **Drainage inspection reminder** - schedule an annual canal and lateral-drain check on all blocks with a root-rot history.
- **Planting-material check** - flag any planting round on affected blocks that skips the pre-plant dip.
