Rice gall midge (_Orseolia oryzae_ in Asia, _O. oryzivora_ in Africa) larvae feed inside the growing point of tillers. The plant produces a tubular, hollow, silver-white "silver shoot" (also called onion leaf or ginger shoot) instead of a normal leaf. Affected tillers never produce a panicle. High-yielding varieties without a _Gm_ resistance gene can lose more than 60% of tillers in outbreak conditions.

## When rice is most vulnerable

Tillering. Once panicle initiation has passed, new gall-midge attack cannot damage yield (the panicle is already committed).

## How gall midge shows in satellite data

- **Reduced tillering** across affected zones shows as lower-than-expected NDVI during tillering.
- Signature overlaps with N deficiency, poor soil, and zinc deficiency; separate with ground scouting for silver shoots.
- Once heading passes and non-panicle tillers are visible, the yield loss is already locked in.

<Warning>
  Gall midge looks like nutrient deficiency from above. If a top-dressing does not green up the affected zones and silver shoots are visible on scouting, suspect gall midge.
</Warning>

## Field symptoms

- Silver-white, tubular, hollow "silver shoot" replacing a normal leaf on affected tillers
- Affected tillers do not produce a panicle
- Reduced total tillering, uneven canopy height
- Damage is heaviest in shaded, humid, weedy plots and in staggered-planting areas

## Reduce the damage by

- **Grow resistant varieties** carrying _Gm_ genes - by far the most effective control.
- **Synchronized planting** across a whole area breaks the continuous breeding cycle.
- **Destroy volunteer rice and grassy weeds** on bunds and fallow between seasons.
- **Reduce shading and improve drainage** where possible.
- **Selective insecticide** (chlorantraniliprole or carbofuran, where permitted) only if scouting justifies it during tillering.
- **Do not rely on late-season insecticide** - once silver shoots appear, damage is already done.

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. Focus alerts on the tillering window so silver shoots are caught while additional damage can still be prevented.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for gall midge on rice:

- **NDVI threshold alert** during tillering on susceptible varieties.
- **New Imagery alert** in the tillering window on fields with a history of gall midge.
