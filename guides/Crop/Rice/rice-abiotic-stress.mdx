When an alert fires on a rice field, the platform tells you _something changed_. This guide helps you decide _what changed and what to do_. Use it as the bridge between a triggered [alert](/guides/alerts-notifications) and a field action.

## What this guide covers

Rice diagnosis is organized around three pillars, matching the decision tree a scout actually uses in the field.

  <Card title="Abiotic Stress" icon="sun" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-abiotic-stress/rice-drought">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Nutrient Deficiency" icon="flask" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/nitrogen">
    N, P, K, Zn, S, and iron toxicity. Highest platform value: NDRE is designed for this.

  <Card title="Biotic Stress" icon="bug" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/blast">

## How to use the three pillars

Use the pattern in the imagery to decide which pillar to open first:

| What you see | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Whole field or whole zones stressed after weather event | Abiotic stress |
| Uniform yellowing without a clear environment cause | Nutrient deficiency |
| Patchy, spreading, or symptoms concentrated on individual plants | Biotic stress |
| A field that never green-fills despite adequate inputs | Check all three - overlapping causes are common |

## Growth-stage vulnerability

Different stresses hurt rice at different growth stages. Match the stage to the likely cause before opening a specific stress page.

| Growth stage | Most damaging causes |
| --- | --- |
| Seedling / transplanting | Salinity, submergence, zinc deficiency, bakanae, tungro |
| Tillering | Drought, poor soil, N and P deficiency, gall midge, sheath blight |
| Booting to flowering | Drought (highest yield impact), salinity, K deficiency, blast, stem borer, false smut |
| Grain filling | Drought, submergence, brown planthopper, rice bug, sheath blight |

## The three pillars

### Abiotic stress

Environmental causes account for the majority of yield loss worldwide and show clearly in satellite imagery. Start here when the pattern follows topography, water flow, or a weather event.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Drought" icon="sun" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-abiotic-stress/rice-drought">
    NDWI drops before NDVI. Leaf rolling, stunted growth, burning leaf tips, whiteheads at flowering.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Salinity" icon="droplet" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-abiotic-stress/rice-salinity">
    Patchy NDVI in low-lying or coastal zones. Brown leaf tips, chlorotic patches, white panicle branches.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Poor soil / low fertility" icon="layer-group" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-abiotic-stress/rice-poor-soil">
    Persistently low NDVI and NDRE across the whole field or repeatable weak zones season after season.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Submergence" icon="water" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-abiotic-stress/rice-submergence">
    Sudden NDVI collapse in flood-prone zones after heavy rain. Full or partial disappearance of canopy.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Nutrient deficiency

Nutrient limitation looks similar to abiotic stress but responds to fertilizer, not to weather or drainage. NDRE is the leading spectral indicator - use it from tillering onward.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Nitrogen" icon="leaf" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/nitrogen">
    Uniform pale-green canopy, older leaves first. Low NDRE.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Phosphorus" icon="seedling" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/phosphorus">
    Dark-green stunted plants, reddish-purple older leaves.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Potassium" icon="wheat-awn" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/potassium">
    Yellow-brown scorching along older leaf margins. Increased lodging.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Zinc" icon="hexagon" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/zinc">
    Patchy dusty-brown blotches at early tillering on high-pH soils.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sulfur" icon="atom" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/sulfur">
    Pale-yellow young leaves (opposite of N). Field does not respond to N top-dressing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Iron toxicity" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/nutrient/iron-toxicity">
    Bronze-orange stippling on lower leaves in acid, waterlogged zones.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Biotic stress

Living causes: diseases, insect pests, and weeds. Satellites narrow _where_ to scout, but ground confirmation is essential before treatment.

<Warning>
  Satellites are excellent at abiotic and good at nutrient. Biotic detection needs ground truth. Always confirm the identification with a scout before applying pesticides.
</Warning>

**Diseases**

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Blast" icon="virus" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/blast">
    Diamond-shaped lesions; neck blast causes whiteheads.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bacterial leaf blight" icon="bacterium" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/bacterial-leaf-blight">
    Yellow wavy lesions from leaf tips; bacterial ooze in morning.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sheath blight" icon="mound" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/sheath-blight">
    Greenish-grey lesions on lower sheaths in dense high-N canopies.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Tungro" icon="virus-covid" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/tungro">
    Yellow-orange stunted plants; transmitted by green leafhopper.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sheath rot" icon="ban" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/sheath-rot">
    Discolored flag-leaf sheath; panicle fails to emerge properly.
  </Card>

  <Card title="False smut" icon="circle-dot" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/false-smut">
    Yellow-green fungal balls replacing grains. Preventive fungicide at booting only.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bakanae" icon="up-long" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/diseases/bakanae">
    Abnormally tall, pale, thin seedlings. Seed-borne.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Insect pests**

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Brown planthopper" icon="bug" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/brown-planthopper">
    Circular hopperburn patches; honeydew at plant base.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stem borers" icon="worm" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/stem-borers">
    Deadhearts at vegetative stage; whiteheads at heading.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Leaffolder" icon="scroll" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/leaffolder">
    Folded leaves with white longitudinal streaks.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Rice bug" icon="mosquito" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/rice-bug">
    Empty, spotted grains at milk stage; strong bug odor.

  <Card title="Gall midge" icon="grip-lines-vertical" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/gall-midge">
    Silver shoots (onion leaves) replacing normal leaves. No panicle on affected tillers.

  <Card title="Armyworms" icon="locust" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/insects/armyworms">
    Sudden defoliation moving in waves; nocturnal.

**Weeds**

  <Card title="Grasses" icon="wheat-awn-circle-exclamation" href="/guides/Crop/Rice/biotic/weeds/grasses">

## Decision flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Note the growth stage of the crop">
    Check the season record for the field. Match the stage to the vulnerability table above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at which index moved and how">
    - NDWI drops first, NDVI follows: think **drought**.
    - NDVI collapses suddenly after rain: think **submergence**.
    - NDVI patchy and stable-low, coastal / low-lying: think **salinity**.
    - Field-scale low NDRE, uniform yellowing: think **N deficiency** (or S if N top-dressing did not help).
    - Circular expanding pale patches: think **hopperburn** (BPH).
    - Rapid, large drop between passes: think **armyworm** or **BLB after a storm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with field symptoms">
    Open the matching stress page and compare the symptom list to what a scout sees on the ground. Symptoms rule stresses in or out faster than imagery alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act, then set an early-warning alert">
    Apply the recommended action. Then use the [Alerts](/guides/alerts-notifications) page to configure a follow-up alert so the same stress is caught earlier next time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
