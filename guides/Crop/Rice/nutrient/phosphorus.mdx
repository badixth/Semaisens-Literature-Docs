Phosphorus deficiency limits root development, tillering, and early crop vigor. It is common on acid upland soils, weathered soils, and cold-water-fed paddies. P is immobile in soil, so early basal placement matters more than for any other nutrient.

## When rice is most vulnerable

Early - from seedling through active tillering. Correction after panicle initiation has almost no yield benefit.

## How phosphorus deficiency shows in satellite data

- **Persistently low NDVI at establishment** on affected zones - the field never reaches the expected early-season NDVI band for its [ecosystem](/guides/Crop/Rice/rice-ecosystems).
- Tillering is reduced, so canopy closure is delayed - visible as low ground cover in mid-season imagery.
- Signature overlaps with poor soil and cold stress; separate them using soil map, water source, and the reddish leaf color on the ground.

## Field symptoms

- Dark-green, narrow, erect leaves (the classic P-deficient look)
- Stunted plants with very few tillers
- Older leaves develop reddish-purple discoloration on cultivars able to produce anthocyanin
- Delayed maturity
- Small panicles and poor grain filling

## Reduce the damage by

- **Basal placement of P fertilizer** at or before transplanting - P does not move in soil.
- **Apply lime on acid soils** (pH below 5.5) to raise P availability.
- **Use P-tolerant cultivars** where soil P is chronically low.
- **Add organic amendments** (compost, farmyard manure) to slowly build available P and reduce P fixation on iron and aluminum oxides.
- **Do not skip starter P** even when soil test values look adequate on newly cleared or long-fallowed land.

## Set an alert for early warning

<Tip>
  Turn the diagnosis into a trigger. Go to the [Alerts](/guides/alerts-notifications) page and configure a rule so weak establishment on P-limited zones is flagged in time to intervene at tillering.
</Tip>

Recommended settings for phosphorus on rice:

- **NDVI threshold alert** at 21 to 30 days after transplanting - trigger when NDVI stays below your ecosystem baseline for two consecutive passes.
- **Seasonal review** of zones that consistently underperform at establishment - correct with basal P next season, not with rescue applications this one.
