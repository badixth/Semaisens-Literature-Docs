Crop pages in this documentation name active ingredients. **A named active is a description of practice, not a clearance to use it.** Registration changes, and this documentation is not the register.

<Warning>
  The platform does not check pesticide registration status. It will not refuse a scout task, a finding, or a prescription because of the active ingredient named in it. **Verify against the register before purchase or application.**
</Warning>

## The authority

Registration in Malaysia is administered by the **Pesticides Board** through the Bahagian Kawalan Racun Perosak dan Baja, Jabatan Pertanian Malaysia.

| Source | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [Senarai racun makhluk perosak yang telah diharamkan dan dihadkan penggunaan](https://www.doa.gov.my/doa/resources/aktiviti_sumber/sumber_awam/maklumat_racun_perosak/pendaftaran_rmp/senarai_racun_perosak_haram_terhad_ogos2025.pdf) | Banned actives, actives with a scheduled end-of-registration date, and actives restricted to named crops. Updated 26 August 2025. |
| [Senarai racun makhluk perosak berdaftar](http://www.portal.doa.gov.my/racunberdaftar/) | The registered-product list, updated monthly. |

## The rule this documentation follows

**No crop page names a banned active as a recommendation.** Where an active was removed because it is banned, the page says so and links here rather than deleting the sentence silently — a reader who has that product in the store needs to know why it is gone.

Where an active is **restricted** rather than banned, the page states the restriction alongside the recommendation. Restricted actives may only be sold against a purchase authorisation issued by the Pesticide Licensing Officer, and may not be displayed for sale.

## Actives removed from crop pages

Recorded so a reader who remembers the earlier recommendation can see what replaced it.

| Active | Status | Effective | Was recommended on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monocrotophos | Registration terminated | 31 December 2025 | Oil palm — bagworms, nettle caterpillars (trunk injection) |
| Chlorpyrifos | Withdrawn from agricultural use, then registration terminated | 2023, then 1 July 2026 | Pineapple — symphylids (in-furrow) |
| Carbofuran | Banned | 2023 | Rice — gall midge |
| Paraquat | Banned | 2020 | Pineapple — weeds (post-emergence) |
| Methomyl | Banned | 1974 | Pineapple — thrips (resistance rotation) |
| Butachlor | Banned | — | Rice — grass weeds, broadleaf weeds (pre-emergence) |

## Restricted actives still named

| Active | Restriction | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acephate | Insect control on coconut and oil palm only, since 2005 | No end date set |
| Methamidophos | Trunk injection on coconut and oil palm only, since 1986 | Registration terminates 31 December 2027 |

<Note>
  Methamidophos is listed for completeness and is **not** recommended on any crop page. Its registration ends in 2027, so a trunk-injection programme built on it has no future.
</Note>
