Google connects seamlessly to the tools you already rely on — from farm management software and equipment telematics to weather data providers and custom-built workflows. Whether you want a one-click sync with John Deere Operations Center or a fully custom integration via the REST API, Google fits into your existing precision agriculture stack without requiring you to change how you work.

## Subscription Requirements

<Note>
  Some integrations are available on all plans, while others require a Pro or
  Enterprise subscription. Equipment telematics connectors (John Deere, AGCO,
  CNH), personal weather station connections, and ISO-XML prescription exports
  are available on **Pro and Enterprise plans only**. Visit
  **Settings → Billing** for a full breakdown of what is included at each
  tier.
</Note>

## Setting Up an Integration

To browse and activate available connectors, navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the Google dashboard. Each connector card shows its current status, the data it exchanges, and any authentication steps required. Select a connector to begin the setup flow — most take fewer than five minutes to configure.

<Tip>
  If you manage multiple farm operations or organizations, you can activate the
  same integration separately for each organization. Navigate to the
  organization selector in the top navigation bar before opening
  **Settings → Integrations** to ensure you are configuring the correct account.
</Tip>
