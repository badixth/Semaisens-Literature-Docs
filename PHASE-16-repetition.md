**A dense broadsheet news page is about 1,000 words.** `What needs you` is nearly two of them, on one screen, in an operational queue somebody is meant to work through under time pressure.

**But the shape of the weight is the finding.** On `What needs you`, only **594 of 1,873 words** sit in blocks of 28 words or more. **Two-thirds of the text is short fragments accumulating across items.**

That matters because Phase 12 attacked long method paragraphs and it worked — National overview's long blocks are down to 434 words across 10 blocks, and *"a band is a definition rather than a measurement"* is gone from every surface. **The weight that remains has a different cause and will not yield to the same fix.**

## 2 · The rule

> **A sentence is written once, at the level it is true for.**

If it is true of every item in a list, it belongs to the list, not to each item. If it is true on four screens, it belongs to one of them and the others link. If it is true of the platform rather than of this figure, it belongs in the trace.

This is the standing convention — *anything the resolver computes has exactly one reader* — applied to prose instead of to values. **The project has paid for the value version of this five times.**

## 3 · Three causes, measured, with the fix for each

### 3.1 The same sentence shape on every item

`What needs you` renders **22 items**. Ten of them carry their own effect sentence:

> *"23–41% of grain yield at risk · act today · 5–9 t of an expected 21.8 t"*

Three carry a full provenance paragraph, 62 words each:

> *"Seen 14 Jun, 18:40 · logged 14 Jun, 22:15 by Ali Ismail. The block was at tillering when it was seen. Delivered on the safety-floor bound, unconfirmed at 4 hours. Nobody has confirmed it, so it went to the agronomist pool at 4 hours."*

**One instance of that is good writing. Three is a wall, and ten of the effect line is wallpaper.**

**Fix.** The item keeps what distinguishes it from its neighbours — the field, the severity, the window, the one thing to do. **Everything true of the class moves to the group header, and everything about how this item reached you moves behind the item's own disclosure.** The reader who wants to know why Ali's finding went to the agronomist pool can open it; the reader scanning twenty-two rows should not have to read it twenty-two times.

**Keep the effect figure. Cut its scaffolding.** *"23–41% at risk · 5–9 t of 21.8 t"* says the same thing as the sentence above it in a third of the words.

### 3.2 The same block on more than one screen

Rendered **verbatim on both `National overview` and `How a figure is built`**, 55 words:

> *"A count of fields, never a severity for the group. No estate on this page carries a band of its own. A field carrying more than one finding at those bands is one field here — the 17 findings behind these 16 fields are counted where the denominator is findings, which is not this figure."*

**Fix.** It lives on **`How a figure is built`**, which exists to explain how a figure is built. The overview links to it. **A sentence has one home.**

**Sweep for others.** Any block of 25+ words appearing on more than one screen is a duplicate by definition — find them by comparing rendered text across all sixteen, not by memory.

### 3.3 The platform explaining itself in the item

Several sentences describe **how the platform behaves** rather than what is true of this field:

> *"Coverage is said first, because a green reading on ground that has not reported is a reading with a date on it rather than an answer."*
> *"Above its band and below it are drawn in one colour, because both are distance from the band and neither is the milder direction."*
> *"A region that pools several schemes is a group nobody asked to be in, so one holding fewer than 5 fields is counted in the totals and not named."*

**Every one of these is right, and none of them is about the estate in front of the reader.** They are the product teaching its own method — the same finding Phase 12 named, in a place Phase 12 did not look.

**Fix: trace or docs**, per the three fates. The reader who doubts the colour choice or the k rule goes looking; the reader reading their estate should not have to walk past it.

**One exception, and hold it firmly:** where the sentence changes what the reader believes **about the figure beside it**, it stays. *"268.4 ha of Kelantan has not reported for 10 days, so the position above is that last reading rather than today's"* is not method — it is the reason the zero above it is not good news.

## 5 · What must not be cut, at any word count

**Read this before deleting anything.** A density pass is exactly where these disappear, and a lost refusal will not be noticed by a word count.

- **Every refusal, and its reason.** *"No combined figure"*, *"no total across crops"*, *"1 of 5 estates has written one · no total across them"*, *"3 rate zones off — no base rate yet"*, the pineapple open-batch sentence, and the VRA fail-closed lines.
- **Every coverage gap, with its date.** *"One scheme has not reported since 5 June"*, *"synced 10 d ago"*, and the Kelantan caveat that explains its zero.
- **Every unit**, and every k-suppression with the total that survives it.
- **Band position and saturation** — inside, above, below, with hectares, and *"saturated, so this figure ranks against nothing"*.
- **The attribution sentences on VRA maps** — *"Semai proposes the rates; a person decides them"*, and *"Drafted by Ali Ismail, confirmed by Ali Ismail, approved by Siti Rahman."*
- **Every proof-pack refusal**, including that a pack failing its check cannot be sent.
- **The gauges** — 15 on National overview, 15 on How a figure is built, 12 on Fields workspace, 1 on the field detail.

**The test for a sentence:** *would a reader draw a wrong conclusion without it?* If yes it stays, however long the screen. Only *"is this the third time the screen has told me this"* justifies a cut.

## 6 · Acceptance tests

**Both halves must pass. Either alone is meaningless.**

**Weight**

1. Every operational screen is **under 600 words** of body text, rail excluded, at 1440.
2. Every reading surface is **under 900**.
3. `What needs you` is under 600 — down from 1,873.
4. **No block of 25+ words appears on more than one screen.** Compare rendered text across all sixteen.
5. No item in a list carries a sentence that is true of every item in that list.

**Preservation — measured against the current build, not from memory**

6. Every refusal listed in §5 still renders, with its reason.
7. Every coverage gap still renders, with its date.
8. Every figure is unchanged. **Diff every number on every screen.**
9. Gauge counts unchanged: 15 · 15 · 12 · 1.
10. Zero bare `title` attributes. Nothing moved behind hover to meet a word count.
11. No refusal was shortened into ambiguity. **A refusal may lose adjectives; it may not lose its reason.**

**Unchanged elsewhere**

12. At **390 · 834 · 1440**: zero horizontal overflow, zero clipped text, zero controls under 44 px at 390.
13. Colour passes AA in both themes.
14. Every control that worked before still works — the field picker, the plan list, the pack list, the board, both creation surfaces.

**Method:** measure, do not look. Allow **2 seconds** after navigating. **Before recording an absence, confirm the probe detects that thing where it is known to exist** — four false findings in this project came from probes that could not have returned a positive.
